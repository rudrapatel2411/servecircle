# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - ServeCircle
*(Comprehensive Version: User Concepts + Business Plan PDF)*

## 1. Introduction
**ServeCircle** is an all-in-one digital service ecosystem connecting customers with verified skilled workers. From home repairs to events, and cleaning to employment, it provides a seamless and transparent solution for all service needs. 

*Disclaimer: ServeCircle is an independent platform and has absolutely no relation to the Craverise website.*



## 2. Platform Panels (User Interfaces)
The ServeCircle ecosystem operates across four dedicated panels:

### 2.1. Customer Panel 👤
*Kon use karega: Jo log services book karte hain*
- **Register / Login:** Secure authentication using Phone/OTP/Email.
- **Service Browse & Book:** Access to 50+ services across 8 categories. AI-based smart diagnosis for instant cost estimates.
- **Live Worker Tracking:** Uber-like real-time GPS tracking of the assigned professional on a live map.
- **Payment & Wallet:** Integrated digital wallet, multiple payment gateways, and pay-after-satisfaction.
- **Booking History:** Detailed logs of past, active, and upcoming bookings with invoice downloads.
- **Reviews & Ratings:** Ability to rate workers and leave detailed reviews.
- **Subscription Plan Manage:** Opt-in and manage Free, Silver, Gold, or Platinum recurring plans.
- **Notifications & Reminders:** Push alerts for booking updates, warranty reminders (Smart Warranty Tracker), and offers.
- **Additional Tools:** SOS Safety Button, Paid Video Consultations before booking.

### 2.2. Worker Panel 👷
*Kon use karega: Jo log kaam karte hain platform pe*
- **Register / Login (ID proof + skill select):** Simple onboarding with document upload, background check, and skill selection.
- **Job Requests Accept/Reject:** Ability to accept or negotiate incoming requests based on location and payout.
- **Aaj Ka Schedule Dekhna:** Daily and weekly calendar view for shifts and jobs.
- **Earnings Track Karna:** Real-time digital dashboard to track daily earnings, commissions, and pending payouts.
- **Free Training Videos:** Access to skill-upgrade tutorials tied to Govt's PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana.
- **Certificate Download:** Digital experience certificates and "ServeCircle Verified Pro" badge (unlocked after 50 jobs).
- **Profile Manage:** Manage service portfolio, and add "Before/After" gallery photos to showcase work.
- **Customer Se Chat Karna:** In-app secure chat and masked calling to communicate safely with the customer.

### 2.3. Admin Panel 🏢
*Kon use karega: ServeCircle ka owner / team*
- **Saare Bookings Dekhna:** Real-time monitoring of all active bookings with manual intervention and worker reassignment tools.
- **Workers Verify & Approve:** Dashboard to verify KYC documents and approve/reject worker profiles.
- **Revenue & Analytics Dekhna:** View platform earnings, active users, and business growth metrics.
- **Complaints Handle Karna:** Dispute resolution ticketing system for refunds and customer/worker issues.
- **Coupons / Offers Banana:** Create and distribute promotional codes and discount campaigns.
- **Subscription Manage:** Configure and monitor user/B2B subscription plans.
- **NGO Aur B2B Partners Manage:** Handle tie-ups with NGOs and approve corporate clients.
- **Notifications Bhejana:** Send bulk broadcast notifications or alerts to specific workers or customers.

### 2.4. B2B / Partner Panel 🤝
*Kon use karega: Housing societies, offices, builders, NGOs*
- **Bulk Service Booking:** Group bookings for societies/offices with bulk discount pricing.
- **Monthly Contract Manage:** Setup and manage long-term Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
- **Multiple Locations Add:** Manage services across various office branches or society blocks from one account.
- **Team Ke Liye Alag Accounts:** Role-based access with child accounts for managers or staff.
- **Invoice Aur Billing Dekhna:** Consolidated monthly enterprise billing and payment tracking.
- **Dedicated Account Manager Se Contact:** Direct chat/contact module for priority B2B support.

## 3. The 8 Main Service Categories
1. **Home Repairs:** Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, AC Repair, Painter. 
2. **Vehicle Services:** Doorstep Car/Bike repair, washing, puncture, denting, emergency jumpstart.
3. **Cleaning & Hygiene:** Deep clean, pest control, sofa wash, water tank cleaning.
4. **Events & Celebrations:** Full event planning (Birthday, Griha Pravesh, Diwali), decorator, caterer, DJ, photography.
5. **Furniture & Decor:** Assembly, polish, interior consultation, wallpaper.
6. **Garden & Outdoor:** Plant care, lawn maintenance, gate/grill repair.
7. **Emergency Services:** 24/7 priority help (30-min response) for water leakage, short circuit, lock break-in, vehicle breakdown.
8. **Employment & Opportunities:** Connecting unemployed skilled youth, women (tailoring/cooking), and daily wage workers with jobs.

---

## 4. Advanced Technical Features
- **AI Smart Diagnosis:** Customers upload photos/videos to get instant AI cost estimates and filter out fake service calls.
- **HomeFixr Score:** AI-based health score for homes based on maintenance history and pending services.
- **Smart Warranty Tracker:** Appliance registration that automatically alerts users when a service is due.
- **Before/After Gallery:** Visual proof of work uploaded by workers for customers to see.
- **Group Booking:** Societies can book workers for multiple flats at once to save worker travel time and get discounts.

---

## 5. Subscription Plans
1. **Basic (Free):** Standard pricing, basic support, history tracking.
2. **Silver (₹199/month):** 10% discount, priority booking, 1 free consultation/month.
3. **Gold (₹499/month):** 20% discount, priority emergency response, free quarterly AC/geyser check, warranty tracker, 2 video consultations/month.
4. **Platinum (₹999/month):** 30% discount, dedicated personal manager, unlimited consultations, free annual home health audit, 15-min VIP emergency response, B2B pricing.

---

## 6. Revenue Model
- **Booking Commission:** ~35% of total revenue (15-25% cut per booking).
- **Subscription Fees:** ~25% from Silver/Gold/Platinum users.
- **Featured Worker Listing:** ~12% from workers paying for premium visibility.
- **Emergency Premium:** ~10% from 2x fees on 24/7 emergency jobs.
- **Event Packages:** ~8% flat fee on complete event planning.
- **B2B Contracts:** ~6% from housing societies and corporate offices.
- **In-App Advertising:** ~4% from local hardware/brand ads.

---

## 7. Social Impact & Worker Growth
- **First Job Initiative:** Freshers pay 0% commission on their first 5 jobs.
- **Women Empowerment:** Safe platform for women to offer home-based services like tailoring, cooking, and tuition.
- **Local Hero Program:** Top workers get monthly awards, bonuses, and local media recognition.
- **NGO & Govt Tie-ups:** Partnership with PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana and NGOs for training underprivileged youth.
